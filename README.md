# deen-hub
Deen Seekho" (दीन सीखो) ek complete, modern aur authentic Islamic educational web platform hai, jise khaaskar bacchon, beginners, buzurgon aur family ke sabhi members ke liye deen ki buniyadi taleem ko aasaan banane ke maqsad se design kiya gaya hai. Iska visual theme shant, adab-o-ehtiram se bharpoor aur spiritual feel deta hai,.
Core Features aur Learning Modules:Alif-Ba Alphabet Module: Isme Alif se lekar Yaa tak pure 28 Arabic huroof shamil hain. 
Har harf ke saath uska arbi matn, aasan Hindi talaffuz, Roman lipi aur beginner-friendly makhraj notes diye gaye hain.
Saath hi har harf ke 4 connecting forms (Isolated, Initial, Medial, Final) aur tracing canvas bhi maujood hai.  
Harakat aur Huroof Jodna: Zabar, Zer, Pesh, Jazm (Sukoon), Tashdeed aur Tanween ke qawaid misalon ke saath samjhaye gaye hain. Live letter joining workbench ke zariye right-to-left shabdon ko jodkar seekhne ki interactive suvidha hai. 
Mukammal Namaz Guide: Paanchon waqt ki namaz (Fajr se Isha) ka mukammal rakat chart (Farz, Sunnah, Witr) diya gaya hai. Niyyah, Takbeer, Sana, Ruku, Qauma, Sajdah, Tashahhud aur Salam ko step-by-step Arabic, transliteration aur Hindi tarjume ke saath darj kiya gaya hai. 
Wuzu aur Ghusl: Wuzu ke 4 Farz, Ghusl ke 3 Farz, sunnat tareeqe aur nawaqid-e-wuzu (wuzu todne wali cheezein) authentic ahadees ke hawale se diye gaye hain.  
Short Surahs aur 6 Kalme: Surah Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas samet 6 Kalme aur Iman-e-Mujmal/Mufassal verified sanad ke saath shamil hain. 
Masnoon Duas aur Digital Tasbih: Rozmarrah ki 30 ahem duayein aur sound/vibration support ke saath ek digital tasbih counter diya gaya hai.
Isme bina kisi third-party backend ke LocalStorage par progress aur bookmarks save hote hain. Har deeni content ke saath verified source, status aur error report karne ki suvidha maujood hai.
